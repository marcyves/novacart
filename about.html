Reliable tech for everyday Europeans

NovaCart is a mid-size D2C shop: earbuds, chargers, smart plugs, laptop sleeves. Designed in Rotterdam, packed in Liège, sold on this site and on two marketplaces. Mid-range on purpose — we do not chase flagship margins and we do not sell no-name trays.

The brief we give ourselves: something you can lose on a train and replace without a finance meeting. USB-C. EU plugs. A warranty that is actually two years.

Shipping & returns

We ship to France, Belgium, the Netherlands and Germany. Two to four working days from Liège. Free from €50, otherwise €4.90. Returns within 30 days, unused, in the mailer they arrived in.

This storefront is a fictional shop used in the IPAG MSc course WEB Tools. There is no real checkout, no customer database, and no warehouse in Liège.
